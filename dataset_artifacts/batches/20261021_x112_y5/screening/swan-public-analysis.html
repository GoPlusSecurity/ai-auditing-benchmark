SWAN TREASURY (STY) Exploit Analysis

Date: 2026-07-30

Loss Estimate: 756,076.767 USDT gross outflow (~$756,077), including 740,275.767 USDT from the STY/USDT Pancake pair. The attacker withdrew 625,246.924 USDT after fees and routing side effects.

I. Executive Summary

  • Verdict: Confirmed Exploit - discounted signed-buy / AMM liquidity drain.
  • Root Cause: ZhaiquanBuy.buy() at 0x27af47FA5681E4c4EF5FFDcfF47EB0DD3Bac6a8C accepted a signer-approved order with discount=1 and used getBuyamount() to multiply the live AMM-priced purchase by 100 / discount. There was no on-chain lower bound for discount, cap on the resulting STY amount, resale lock, or liquidity-impact guard. In the focal transaction the formula priced STY at 2.8743216139 USDT/STY and transferred 687,245 STY for only 19,753.631575 USDT; the attacker immediately dumped those tokens into the STY/USDT pair.

The recovered signer was the contract's configured signer 0xdEb4E4ed40E8d29d10A326Fb4652fDaa55438284. On-chain evidence proves signer authorization occurred; it does not prove whether the signer key/API was compromised or whether the bad order was intentionally issued.

II. Vulnerability

A. Business Logic Context

STY purchases were routed through ZhaiquanBuy, which receives USDT and transfers STY inventory to the caller. The STY amount is calculated from the token's current Pancake reserve price via STYTOKEN.getCurrentPrice().

The STY token itself permits immediate transfers back into the Pancake pair. Its sell path takes fee/profit/burn portions and calls a FeeCutter, which can perform nested token-for-USDT swaps during the sale. Those hooks redistributed part of the value but did not prevent the AMM from being drained.

The violated invariant is that discounted sale inventory should not be convertible into AMM liquidity at a price far below market in the same transaction. With discount=1, the source formula mechanically computes a 100x token multiplier; the contract does not impose any independent on-chain sanity check.

C. Why It Was Exploitable

The attack contract could flash-borrow the USDT needed for the purchase, call buy() with a valid signer message, receive a very large STY balance, and sell immediately. buy() uses msg.sender as the signed user, so the attack contract itself was the authorized buyer. It tracks orderBuiedCount but only against the signed times; for this order, times=1, so a single atomic purchase was enough.

The STY/USDT pair had deep USDT liquidity before the dump: the trace showed reserves of 1,786,627.651919 USDT and 621,582.373829 STY before the attacker's sale sequence. That made the discounted STY inventory directly cashable.

III. Technical Attack Flow

  1. The EOA 0x840fF67CDd5Bc3cf51eBF758C35fEaAC92cDada4 called attack contract 0xCbAFd579210f2beE8e618C2166a87d927dFAadFB in the focal transaction.
  2. The attack contract called unverified helper 0x758905b399f4216E9e3fF791EEa16c05c91A1a88; the trace shows ecrecover returning the configured signer and then 0x758905... transferring 184,198 STY to the attack contract.
  3. A second helper, 0x473aea0Ce9292503CEB9Bbef71B2441656F7c7cF, transferred 15,801 USDT to the attack contract after another signer recovery. This contract is unverified, so only the observed transfer is used as evidence.
  4. The attack contract flash-swapped 19,753.631575491225493808 USDT from Pancake pair 0x16b9a82891338f9bA80E2D6970FddA79D1eb0daE.
  5. Inside the flash callback, it called ZhaiquanBuy.buy(orderid=999888777, totalamount=19753.631575 USDT, times=1, discount=1, cooldown=0, signedmsg=...). The trace shows ecrecover returning 0xdEb4E4ed40E8d29d10A326Fb4652fDaa55438284, then transferFrom of 19,753.631575 USDT to 0x72C49724FD7Cf2CF3a25D1d16D4F82aB5AEf928D, and transfer of 687,245 STY from ZhaiquanBuy to the attack contract.
  6. The attack contract sold STY through Pancake router 0x10ED43C718714eb63d5aA57B78B54704E256024E. The first sale requested 26,143.29 STY and returned 47,232.286128 USDT. Token fee hooks sent fee portions to the FeeCutter path, which performed additional swaps and reward transfers.
  7. The attack contract repaid the flash swap with 19,803.139424051353878505 USDT, leaving the flash pair up by 49.507849 USDT.
  8. The attack contract sold the remaining 845,299.71 STY. The final pair swap sent 582,016.777374 USDT to the attack contract. The STY/USDT pair's final balance delta was -740,275.767029535306546519 USDT and +440,950.158 STY.
