LULA Recycle Price Manipulation Exploit Analysis

Date: 2026-07-28

Loss Estimate: 591,659.751036 USDT drained from the LULA/USDT PancakeSwap V2 pair, about $591,056 at the in-transaction USDT oracle price. The attacker kept 578,295.907588 USDT after 13,363.843449 USDT of flash fees.

I. Executive Summary

  • Verdict: Confirmed Exploit - AMM reserve manipulation through LULA's reward/recycle path.
  • Root Cause: The verified LULA token at 0xF5d7029eb6751d170dcF0Bb1c87Af6f93d5A2e9a exposes recycle(uint256), callable by its rentalContract, which transfers LULA directly out of the PancakeSwap pair and immediately calls sync(). In the focal transaction, the attacker used flash liquidity to fill the pair with USDT, then routed repeated reward claims through the unverified rental contract 0x377a015f44C3FDf71060e94648EDC9e0316C7f1a. Each claim invoked recycle(), shrinking the pair's LULA reserve while the USDT reserve stayed high, so a final LULA sell extracted almost the entire temporary USDT reserve.

II. Vulnerability

A. Business Logic Context

LULA trades against USDT in PancakeSwap V2 pair 0xF0b36389a12A28be1280c0ec2A4bbc76889D6a96. Its token code includes sell taxes, automatic liquidity handling, and a rental/reward subsystem. A PancakeSwap V2 pair prices swaps from its synced token reserves, so reserve balances should not be changed by an external reward path without a corresponding swap or liquidity action.

The rental contract is unverified; get_source_code_list returned no source for 0x377a015f44C3FDf71060e94648EDC9e0316C7f1a. The execution trace nevertheless shows it calling LULA.recycle() from claimTeamReward() and claimReferralReward() flows.

B. The Vulnerability

// LULA/LULA.sol @ 0xF5d7029eb6751d170dcF0Bb1c87Af6f93d5A2e9a
function recycle(uint256 amount) external {
    require(msg.sender == rentalContract, "Only Rental");
    uint256 pairBalance = balanceOf[uniswapV2Pair];
    uint256 maxTake = pairBalance / 3;
    uint256 actual = amount >= maxTake ? maxTake : amount;
    if (actual == 0) return;
    _basicTransfer(uniswapV2Pair, rentalContract, actual);
    IUniswapV2Pair(uniswapV2Pair).sync();
}

This function lets the authorized rental contract remove up to one-third of the pair's LULA balance and then force the AMM to accept the reduced balance as the new reserve. Repeating the call exponentially depletes the LULA reserve without removing USDT, violating the pair's pricing invariant.

C. Why It Was Exploitable

The exploitable precondition was that reward-claim calls could reach recycle() repeatedly in one transaction. After the attacker made the pair hold 197,642,262.105713 USDT and only 24,022.137030 LULA, the repeated recycle() calls reduced the LULA reserve to 0.004352 LULA. At that point, selling 4,749.05 LULA into the pair returned 197,642,080.528989 USDT.

III. Technical Attack Flow

  1. The attacker EOA 0x2677806d48325ced7533c54b86ed5e99b129a4ed called the unverified attack contract 0x5E506Ba06Fa6C61D1069B0E68d7013DE35AFA816 in the focal transaction.
  2. The attack contract assembled temporary capital: 4,403,307.398128 USDT and 407,751.430796 WBNB from 0x8F73..., 14,636,621.772420 USDT through an Aave borrow/repay leg, 64,955,663.379339 USDT through a Venus borrow/repay leg, plus PancakeSwap V3 and pool-manager flash liquidity. The Venus and Aave debts were repaid before transaction end.
  3. Before manipulation, the LULA/USDT pair reserves were 591,841.327761 USDT and 8,001,957.949148 LULA. The attacker first pulled 5,000 LULA from the EOA and sold through the pair, triggering normal sell-tax/liquidity code.
  4. The attack contract then swapped 197,050,771.007709 USDT into the LULA/USDT pair for 7,982,785.687430 LULA. The pair's synced reserves became 197,642,262.105714 USDT and 24,022.137030 LULA.
  5. The contract called helper reward contracts such as 0x2a6C..., 0xF60F..., 0xFE255..., and 0xfD7e.... Those helpers called the rental contract, which repeatedly called LULA.recycle(). Trace examples include recycle(5466425413399659914651) at index 2981, recycle(5549422583180852016752) at index 3089, and later referral-claim recycle calls at indices 3554 and 3703.
  6. Each recycle transferred LULA from the pair to the rental contract and called sync(). The pair's LULA reserve fell from 24,022.137030 LULA to 0.004352 LULA while the USDT reserve remained 197,642,262.105714 USDT.
  7. The attacker sold 4,999 LULA through PancakeSwap; after LULA sell taxes, 4,749.05 LULA reached the pair. The pair sent 197,642,080.528989 USDT to the attack contract.
  8. The attack contract repaid the stacked flash liquidity and lending legs, then transferred 578,295.907588 USDT to the EOA at the end of the focal transaction.
