II. Vulnerability
A. Business Logic Context
NEX is a fee-on-transfer token paired against AIC on a PancakeSwap V2-style pair. Its intended behavior is to take DAO/node fees on sells into automated market-maker pairs while allowing router interactions.
The Pancake pair is standard:
skim(to)
transfers token balances above stored reserves to any recipient, and
sync()
updates reserves to current token balances. Those functions assume token transfers move exactly the requested amount from the pair once.

C. Why It Was Exploitable
The pair already held a large NEX reserve. The helper contract created a NEX surplus just above the pair's reserve, then chose the Pancake router as the
skim
recipient specifically to trigger NEX's router branch. Because the requested skim amount was about half the pair's NEX balance, the double debit did not revert and left the pair with only
1 wei
of NEX.
sync()
then made that manipulated balance the official reserve, breaking the constant-product pricing basis for the next swap.
