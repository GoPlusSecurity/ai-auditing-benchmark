LULA Recycle Price Manipulation Exploit Analysis
Date

Loss Estimate

I. Executive Summary
Verdict
: Confirmed Exploit - AMM reserve manipulation through LULA's reward/recycle path.
Root Cause
: The verified LULA token at
0xF5d7029eb6751d170dcF0Bb1c87Af6f93d5A2e9a
exposes
recycle(uint256)
, callable by its
rentalContract
, which transfers LULA directly out of the PancakeSwap pair and immediately calls
sync()
. In the focal transaction, the attacker used flash liquidity to fill the pair with USDT, then routed repeated reward claims through the unverified rental contract
0x377a015f44C3FDf71060e94648EDC9e0316C7f1a
. Each claim invoked
recycle()
, shrinking the pair's LULA reserve while the USDT reserve stayed high, so a final LULA sell extracted almost the entire temporary USDT reserve.
II. Vulnerability
A. Business Logic Context
LULA trades against USDT in PancakeSwap V2 pair
0xF0b36389a12A28be1280c0ec2A4bbc76889D6a96
. Its token code includes sell taxes, automatic liquidity handling, and a rental/reward subsystem. A PancakeSwap V2 pair prices swaps from its synced token reserves, so reserve balances should not be changed by an external reward path without a corresponding swap or liquidity action.
The rental contract is unverified;
get_source_code_list
returned no source for
0x377a015f44C3FDf71060e94648EDC9e0316C7f1a
. The execution trace nevertheless shows it calling
LULA.recycle()
from
claimTeamReward()
and
claimReferralReward()
flows.
B. The Vulnerability
// LULA/LULA.sol @ 0xF5d7029eb6751d170dcF0Bb1c87Af6f93d5A2e9a

uint256
amount)
external
{
require

);
uint256

uint256

3
;
uint256
actual = amount >= maxTake ? maxTake : amount;
if
(actual ==
0
)
return
;

This function lets the authorized rental contract remove up to one-third of the pair's LULA balance and then force the AMM to accept the reduced balance as the new reserve. Repeating the call exponentially depletes the LULA reserve without removing USDT, violating the pair's pricing invariant.
C. Why It Was Exploitable
The exploitable precondition was that reward-claim calls could reach
recycle()
repeatedly in one transaction. After the attacker made the pair hold 197,642,262.105713 USDT and only 24,022.137030 LULA, the repeated
recycle()
calls reduced the LULA reserve to 0.004352 LULA. At that point, selling 4,749.05 LULA into the pair returned 197,642,080.528989 USDT.

Before manipulation, the LULA/USDT pair reserves were 591,841.327761 USDT and 8,001,957.949148 LULA. The attacker first pulled 5,000 LULA from the EOA and sold through the pair, triggering normal sell-tax/liquidity code.
The attack contract then swapped 197,050,771.007709 USDT into the LULA/USDT pair for 7,982,785.687430 LULA. The pair's synced reserves became 197,642,262.105714 USDT and 24,022.137030 LULA.

The attack contract repaid the stacked flash liquidity and lending legs, then transferred 578,295.907588 USDT to the EOA at the end of the focal transaction.
