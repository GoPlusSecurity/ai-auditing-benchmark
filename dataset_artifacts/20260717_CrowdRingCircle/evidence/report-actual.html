CrowdRingCircle Sell-Destroy Exploit Analysis

Date: 2026-07-16

Loss Estimate: 209,061.632267871026055744 USDT (~$209,061.63), with 201,359.472267252045095696 USDT retained by the attacker EOA after flash/route costs.

I. Executive Summary

  • Verdict: Confirmed Exploit / AMM reserve-manipulation bug.
  • Root Cause: CrowdRingCircle at 0x8581433150f2c48ff2efe5a22b17c7d405054509 overrides _update() so that, when a non-exempt address sells CRC into a configured DEX pair, the token burns CRC from the pair and calls sync() before crediting the seller's CRC to the pair. That lets a transfer into the pair lower the pair reserve mid-swap, violating the AMM accounting assumption and allowing the attacker to drain USDT from the CRC/USDT pair 0xd8799a644850c065388c22df4ee0c28472922526.

II. Vulnerability

A. Business Logic Context

CRC is an ERC-20 with custom transfer restrictions. At the focal block, the CRC/USDT pair was marked isDexPair=true, sellDestroyEnabled=true, sellFeeRate=1000 (10%), and both the EOA 0x34579ea92a07a88f5505dfaa4d99ab94b2784087 and attack contract 0x2eadbcdcd4ab4bb7f8b01610794ea09a64cd5da1 were not exempt. The token owner/treasury 0xa5341a83807503ed8decb8534605494551c2a196 was exempt, so CRC bought from the pair could be routed there despite the buy restriction.

The invariant break is that a sell transfer into the AMM pair first debits the pair's existing CRC balance and synchronizes reserves, then credits the seller's CRC. A router/pair expects token input transfers to increase the input balance, but this hook can lower reserves inside the transfer itself.

C. Why It Was Exploitable

The attacker had a prior CRC allowance path and enough temporary USDT/WBNB liquidity from flash loans. They first used flash liquidity to buy a very large CRC amount out of the pair and route it to the exempt owner address, reducing CRC reserves. Then selling only 45,144 CRC from the non-exempt EOA triggered the sell fee and pair-burn branch, causing the pair to sync() at manipulated reserves and release nearly all USDT liquidity on the following swap.

III. Technical Attack Flow

  1. The EOA deployed helper 0xdb72c97a747424d927b4638b37c9735823bce3a6 in 0x4d5bfe260f0ddccb5ccb28a07a890f0ac69c1d7511f7a3da2b0d73e5bc472634, then approved it for ~100,000 CRC in 0x3194d513bc7717a4b9e4395c36b3d4d9feff6ccbec225482d4d5dab9034dea31.
  2. In the focal transaction 0xeaef22325e02ac65a8e1f2e1a3a43f7b7ac8d2323ce6f698a90813e77017c834, the EOA deployed 0x2eadbcdcd4ab4bb7f8b01610794ea09a64cd5da1. The focused trace shows nested flashLoan, Venus mint/borrow, and Pancake V3 flash calls that assembled large temporary USDT liquidity.
  3. The attack contract called Pancake Router swapTokensForExactTokens, transferring 184,995,877.105807691589020575 USDT into the CRC/USDT pair and moving 35,862,714.912768051805032649 CRC from the pair to the exempt owner/treasury address.
  4. The helper pulled 45,144 CRC from the EOA. The sell transfer charged a 4,514.4 CRC fee to 0xd89e4e97345fa350462ab24d826c8160acbee030, burned/synced 40,629.6 CRC from the pair, then credited 40,629.6 CRC to the pair.
  5. The following fee-on-transfer-supporting router swap caused the pair to send 185,204,938.738075562615076319 USDT to the attack contract. After repaying Venus and the nested flash pools, the attack contract transferred 201,359.472267252045095696 USDT to the EOA.
